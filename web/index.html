Drone World

An open-source drone flight sandbox. Fly over a 1 × 1 km practice area with forest, a lake, an airfield and a gate course. The physics is a simplified quadcopter model: the numbers are estimates, not a certified simulator.

Gamepad and RC radio

Gamepad: left stick throttle and yaw, right stick pitch and roll (mode 2). Y = mode, X = reset, B = camera.

RC radio: plug it in by USB and choose USB Joystick (HID) on the radio (EdgeTX / OpenTX). If the sticks are mixed up, open Settings → Radio and use Learn.

Flight modes

Hold: release the sticks and the drone stops and hovers. Start here.
Angle: self-levelling, you control the throttle.
Acro: the sticks set rotation speed, as FPV pilots fly.
