Drone World

An open-source drone flight sandbox. Fly over a 1 × 1 km practice area with forest, a lake, an airfield, a farm and a gate course, by day or night, in sun, fog, rain, storms or snow. The wind changes with height and gusts, and trees and buildings shelter it. The drone physics is a simplified quadcopter model; its numbers are estimates, not a certified simulator.

Gamepad and RC radio

Gamepad: left stick throttle and yaw, right stick pitch and roll (mode 2). Y = mode, X = reset, B = camera.

RC radio: plug it in by USB and choose USB Joystick (HID) on the radio (EdgeTX / OpenTX). If the sticks are mixed up, open Settings → Radio and use Learn.

Weather and time

Settings → World: time of day, date, weather, temperature and wind. The windsock by the runway is fully stretched out at 15 knots (7.7 m/s).

Graphics

Low for phones and old laptops, High for a gaming PC, Ultra for a strong graphics card. If frames get slow, the game first switches off extras (reflections, glow, some grass, shadow detail) and then lowers the resolution.
